'Open Questions in Open Science' is a live panel discussion series where I invite expert academics with multiple perspectives to discuss some of the biggest and most controversial issues in science. Audience members submit questions and vote on which academic convinced them most.

This series has just begun but I will soon populate this page with all recordings.

### Should peer reviewers be paid?

Scientific progress relies on the “billion-dollar donation” of unpaid peer review anonymously volunteered by the academic community. With heavier workloads and unprecedented submission volumes, however, the system is under stress, with review timelines now stretching to months, when appropriate reviewers can be found at all. Everyone agrees something must change, but what that change should look like remains an open question.

In this panel discussion, three experts will weigh the pros and cons of introducing payment into the peer review pipeline. We will be joined by Associate Professor Daniel Gorelick, spearhead of Biology Open’s Fast & Fair paid peer review scheme, Professor Angela Moles, advocate of the Review by Reciprocity credit system, and Professor Alex Holcombe, open science campaigner for the demonetisation and decentralisation of academic publishing.
